Good to know

Flood is separate. Hurricane deductibles apply to wind damage. Storm surge and rising water need a flood policy, with its own deductible.

You can often choose. Many carriers let you lower the percentage for a higher premium. On the coast, a lower option may not be offered.

When it applies depends on your policy wording, usually a hurricane watch or warning from the National Weather Service. Your declarations page shows which deductible you have.
