Good to know

Small claims can cost you later. Filing a claim for a few hundred dollars can raise your premium for years. Many people pick a higher deductible and save their policy for the big stuff.

Home and auto are separate. Each policy has its own deductible, and your home may also have a separate hurricane deductible.

Only raise it if you can pay it. The right deductible is one you could cover without a credit card.
