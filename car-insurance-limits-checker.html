Are my car insurance limits enough?

South Carolina's minimum is 25/50/25. If you cause a serious wreck, anything above your limits can come out of your own pocket. Enter roughly what you have to protect.

What 25/50/25 means

NumberWhat it paysSC minimum
25Injuries to one person you hurt$25,000
50Injuries to everyone you hurt in one wreck$50,000
25Damage to other people's cars and property$25,000

South Carolina also requires uninsured motorist coverage at the same 25/50/25 minimum. That protects you when the other driver has no insurance. Underinsured motorist coverage, which helps when they don't have enough, is optional and worth asking about. A typical hospital stay after a serious crash can easily pass $25,000.
